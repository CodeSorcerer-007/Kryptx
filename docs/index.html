🛡️ Kryptx Security

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026 • Effective Date: August 16, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: Kryptx is a 100% offline-first, zero-knowledge password vault. We do not own servers, collect zero data, and have zero access to your master password or encryption keys.

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Kryptx operates entirely on your device with no cloud backends:

  • Zero Plaintext Storage: We do not collect, view, transmit, or log passwords, credentials, notes, payment cards, crypto seed phrases, TOTP secrets, or file attachments.
  • Local AES-256-GCM Encryption: All items are encrypted on-device with a 256-bit key derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000+ iterations).
  • Zero Analytics & Trackers: Kryptx contains 0 advertising trackers, 0 diagnostic telemetry, and 0 third-party analytics SDKs.

2. Android Device Permissions

Permission Purpose & Scope
USE_BIOMETRIC Used solely for hardware-backed fingerprint / face unlock via Android Keystore. Biometrics remain strictly isolated in your phone's hardware enclave.
CAMERA Used exclusively for the real-time offline viewfinder to scan 2FA QR codes or P2P sync codes. No photos or video are recorded or uploaded.
BIND_AUTOFILL_SERVICE Enables Android system autofill to match domain names and fill passwords directly inside your apps when unlocked.

3. Data Retention & Erasure

Because all data resides strictly inside your phone's encrypted sandboxed storage, you maintain 100% custody:

  • Instant Vault Wipe: You can permanently wipe your entire vault with 1 tap via Settings → Privacy & Security → Erase Vault.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app permanently erases all vault records from the operating system.
