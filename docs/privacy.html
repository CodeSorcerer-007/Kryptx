Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026 • Version: 1.0.0

Fundamental Privacy Guarantee: Kryptx is a 100% offline-first, zero-knowledge password fortress. We do not own cloud servers, collect zero personal telemetry, and possess zero technical ability to access your master password or encryption keys.

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Kryptx operates exclusively within your Android device's hardware sandbox:

  • Zero Plaintext Storage: We do not log, transmit, monitor, or view credentials, notes, payment cards, crypto seed phrases, TOTP keys, or file attachments.
  • Local-Only AES-256-GCM Encryption: All records are encrypted on-device with a 256-bit key derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (600,000+ iterations).
  • Zero Analytics SDKs: Kryptx contains zero advertising trackers, zero telemetry beacons, and zero diagnostic logging SDKs.

2. Android Device Permissions & Scope

Kryptx requests only the strict minimum permissions required to perform cryptographic operations:

Permission Technical Purpose & Security Boundary
USE_BIOMETRIC Used solely for hardware-backed fingerprint / face unlock via Android Keystore StrongBox TEE. Biometric sensor data is isolated in the hardware enclave and never accessible to the app.
CAMERA Used exclusively for the real-time offline viewfinder to scan 2FA TOTP setup codes and local P2P sync codes. No photos or video are recorded to storage or transmitted over networks.
BIND_AUTOFILL_SERVICE Enables the Android system Autofill service to match domain names and auto-fill credentials directly inside your apps when authenticated.

3. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure Rights

Because all data resides strictly inside your phone's encrypted sandboxed storage, you maintain 100% physical and cryptographic custody:

  • 1-Tap Vault Wipe: You can permanently wipe your entire vault database and securely zeroize memory buffers via Settings → Privacy & Security → Erase Vault.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app permanently purges all SQLite databases and Keystore aliases from the operating system.

4. Optional Privacy-Preserving Features

Breach Detection (k-Anonymity): If you manually trigger the Have I Been Pwned scan, Kryptx uses a mathematical k-Anonymity model. Only the first 5 characters of your password's SHA-1 hash are sent. Your plaintext passwords or full hashes are never transmitted.

Local P2P Wi-Fi Sync: Syncing between devices operates exclusively over direct socket connections on your local Wi-Fi or hotspot with ephemeral AES-256 session keys and 6-digit verification PINs.

5. Compliance (GDPR, CCPA & COPPA)

Because Kryptx does not collect, process, or sell any personal information to third parties, we adhere to global privacy frameworks by architectural design. You hold full rights to access, export (JSON/CSV), and delete your records directly on your device.
